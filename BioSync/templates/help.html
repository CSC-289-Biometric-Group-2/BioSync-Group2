How to use BioSync

Click any guide below to expand it.

Go to the Register page
Choose Patient or Caregiver
Fill in your name, DOB & health details
✓ Log in and start tracking
Click Log Reading on the dashboard
Select your metric (Heart Rate, BP, SpO2…)
Enter the value & select your device
✓ Appears in Dashboard & Trends instantly
Go to Documents in the sidebar
Drag & drop your PDF or click Browse Files
Click Upload Selected Files
✓ Readings extracted automatically
Go to Dashboard or My Profile
Find the Link a Caregiver panel
Click Generate Patient Code
✓ Share the code with your caregiver
Generate your patient code (see above)
Share it with your caregiver
They enter it in their caregiver portal
✓ They can view your readings & documents
Go to the Login page
Click Forgot Password
Enter your username & answer your security question
✓ Set your new password
