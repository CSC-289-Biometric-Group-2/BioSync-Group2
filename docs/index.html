BioSync

A personal health tracking app that reads your lab reports and turns them into clear, actionable insights.

CSC 289 · Programming Capstone · Spring 2026 · Group 2

About

BioSync helps individuals and their caregivers stay on top of health trends over time. Users upload PDF lab reports, and the app automatically extracts biometric data — heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels, glucose, and more — then displays trend charts, flags values outside healthy ranges, and sends alerts when something needs attention. Caregivers can link to patients, monitor readings, and upload documents on their behalf, all from a separate portal.

Tech Stack

Python 3.11 Flask SQLite HTML / CSS / JavaScript pdfplumber NumPy pandas ReportLab

Key Features

  • PDF lab report parsing — upload a report and biometric readings are extracted and stored automatically
  • Health trend charts — visualize how your heart rate, blood pressure, glucose, SpO₂, and more change over time
  • Smart health alerts — age- and sex-adjusted notifications flag readings outside safe ranges
  • Caregiver portal — caregivers can link to patients via a unique code, upload documents, and monitor readings
  • Document hub — all uploaded medical documents organized in one place
  • Baseline comparison — detects meaningful changes by comparing recent readings to your personal historical average
  • Manual readings — log biometric values directly without a document upload
