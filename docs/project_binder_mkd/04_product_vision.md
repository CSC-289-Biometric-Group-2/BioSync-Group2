# Product Vision

## 4a. Target Users / Personas

### Representative User 1 — Joy Edwards

**Joy Edwards** is a busy grad student who is interested in staying on top of her health as well as her grades. She uses many different fitness tracking devices and apps to record her vitals and reach her health goals. BioSync would store all this data in one place and take the stress out of prioritizing her wellness.

### Representative User 2 — Jason Spencer

**Jason Spencer** is the caregiver of an elderly patient and would benefit from having his patient's vitals recorded between visits. His elderly patient is not technologically inclined and does not know how to record this information into apps by herself. With BioSync, medical data will be gathered and recorded when Jason isn't with his patient and is readily available for him to access whenever he needs it.

### Representative User 3 — Micah Davis

**Micah Davis** has a chronic illness that requires multiple trips to various specialists. Tracking his vitals was difficult enough before he got sick but now that this information is important to provide to his doctors, he is having issues finding an app that keeps track of all the data he needs. BioSync not only tracks and records a variety of health data but is easy to share with his doctors in and out of appointments.
