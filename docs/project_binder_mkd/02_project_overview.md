# Project Overview

BioSync is a biometric tracking application that brings data collection and analysis to one place.

This application is intended for those in need of keeping track of medical information and vitals. This could include health-conscious individuals or those with chronic illnesses that need to have records of this data and what it could mean. Caregivers such as doctors or nurses can also utilize BioSync to access patient health data.

Currently, health tracking is scattered across different devices and apps. BioSync doesn't specialize in tracking a specific dataset, but a multitude of metrics such as heartrate, blood pressure, blood oxygen level, and more.

BioSync is currently in a semi-usable state, and most of the application features are present in its latest build, not including external tracking devices.
